The problem

Your relationships live in a dozen places, and none of them are yours.

Contacts scatter across phones, inboxes, and a CRM you rent. They decay quietly. And no one can tell you who changed what, or why.

01

Scattered

Gmail, Outlook, your phone, three spreadsheets, a CRM. The same person, five times, never in sync.

02

Decaying

Titles go stale, emails bounce, people move. Nobody's job is to keep it true.

03

Unaccountable

A field changed. By whom? From where? At what confidence? Silence.

04

Rented

Your relationship graph is the most valuable asset you don't actually own.

What Contact-Ops does

One canonical graph of everyone you know, kept honest by agents you supervise.

Import your world once. An agent fleet dedupes, enriches, and connects it, proposing every change for your approval, tracking every value back to its source.

Agents do the busywork

Dedup, enrichment, and voice-match agents propose changes; you approve them in one inbox. Confidence-scored, never silent, never auto-applied above your bar.

Click any field, see why

Every value carries field-level provenance: which source set it, which agent, when, at what confidence. Full history preserved. Nothing is a black box.

A living relationship graph

People, organizations, employment, and the ties between them, explorable in an interactive 3D graph. See the paths between anyone.

Reversible by design

90-day unmerge, append-only audit, tombstones instead of hard deletes. Every agent action is undoable. You are never one click from data loss.

Sync everywhere

CardDAV to iOS and macOS Contacts. Import from Gmail, Microsoft 365, CSV, and vCard. Your people, current on every device.

Agent-native, not bolted on

The MCP server is the product. Anything a human can do in the UI, an agent can do with scopes, and every agent action is auditable by a human.

Multi-tenant & compliant

Postgres row-level security, HIPAA fencing, GDPR/CCPA export and erasure, per-workspace isolation, from day one.

Yours to host

Run it on your own box, your own Keycloak, your own database. Sovereignty is the default, not an enterprise upsell.

Voice & identity

Resolve the same person across meetings, calls, and apps with a shared voiceprint identity, consent-gated and biometric-safe.

Agent-first, human-governed

Agents propose. You approve. Every change is auditable.

No privileged path: the same MCP tool surface powers the UI, your automations, and any AI agent you authorize. Every mutation is confidence-scored. Above your bar it applies; below it, it waits in a review queue with the agent, its confidence, and exactly what it wants to change.
